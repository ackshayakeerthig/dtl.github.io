Threads of Change

"At Threads of Change, we are dedicated to promoting sustainability by connecting people who wish to donate unused clothes with those in need. Our platform also ensures that worn-out fabrics are repurposed or upcycled by fashion designers, reducing textile waste and supporting eco-friendly initiatives."

HOW IT WORKS

Donate Clothes

Give your pre-loved clothes a new purpose by donating them to those in need.

Upcycle Fashion

Connect with designers to transform your old clothes into unique pieces.

Help Others

Support communities by providing essential clothing to those who need it most.

Sustainable Impact

Join the movement to reduce fashion waste and promote circular fashion.
